Spooky Blast NYC Privacy Policy

Last updated November 9, 2026

Spooky Blast NYC is a free game made by David Barton for kids and families. It has no ads, no purchases and no accounts.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, store or send any personal information. It does not use analytics, tracking, advertising or third party services, and it works without an internet connection.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (coins, levels, garage upgrades and any monster you draw) is saved only on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children. There are no links out of the game, no chat and no way to contact other players.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app changes, and the date above will change.
