Spooky Blast NYC

A friendly Halloween driving game for kids and families. Drive a taxi across New York City at night and pop cartoon ghosts into candy and confetti.

Support

Something not working, or have an idea? Email dbartondds+spookyblast@gmail.com and include your device model if you can.

Common questions

How do I play? Tap or hold anywhere to blast candy. Press JUMP to hop over things on the road.

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

Does it need the internet? No. It plays fully offline.

Are there ads or purchases? No. The game is free with no ads, no purchases and no accounts.

Privacy

The game collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
